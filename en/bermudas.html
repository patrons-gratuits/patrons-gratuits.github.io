Safari Bermuda shorts

Two pairs of Bermuda shorts, one for men and one for women, both made to measure. The same base pattern, with default measurements and fly adapted to each.

Safari Bermuda shorts in a few words. This is the short version of safari trousers: straight waistband with belt loops, button fly, side-seam pockets, cargo and back pockets of your choice, cuffs or a plain hem. Wear them with the safari jacket or on their own. The pattern is built on FreeSewing’s Titan trouser block.
