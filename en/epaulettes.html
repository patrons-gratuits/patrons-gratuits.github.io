If your printer shrinks the page despite “100 %” (e.g. a 48 mm square), enter the measured value: the PDF will be enlarged to compensate.

Each epaulette has 2 pieces: cut 2 copies per epaulette (4 for a pair).

Tip: make a first test in inexpensive fabric (an old sheet, for example).


The one-page PDF has the piece at 1:1 scale, without being split into A4 sheets, to be projected onto the fabric (for example with Pattern Projector).

For a successful print or projection, see Printing at the right size.
