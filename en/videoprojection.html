Sewing with a projector

Project your pattern at full size straight onto your fabric: no pages to print, no sheets to tape, no wasted paper. Every pattern on this site works with a projector. The one-page PDF for projectors is optimised to that effect.

A video to get started

One click loads the YouTube player (no-cookie mode); nothing is loaded before.

A clear introduction to pattern projection. Open on YouTube: “How to Use Pattern Projector for Sewing for Beginners!”, Projector Sewing channel.

Why project?

  • No taping: a safari jacket means 25 to 30 A4 pages to print and tape together. With a projector, there is nothing to prepare.
  • No paper: nothing to print, nothing to throw away.
  • More freedom: change an option or a measurement, download the PDF again, and you are ready.
  • Layout: you see every piece on the fabric, so you can place them well and save fabric.

What you need

  • An ordinary projector; it does not need to be high-end.
  • A mount that holds it above your cutting table, pointing down: a stand, an articulated arm or a ceiling mount.
  • A table or cutting surface large enough to lay the fabric flat.
  • A computer or tablet connected to the projector.

Step by step

  1. Download the one-page pattern. On every “Create a pattern” page, the “One-page PDF for projectors” button gives all the pieces at 1:1 scale on a single page.
  2. Open it in the free Pattern Projector app, shown in the video. It displays the pattern at the right scale from your projector’s calibration.
  3. Calibrate. Project the 5 × 5 cm test square and measure it on the table with a ruler: it must be exactly 50 mm. Check it in the centre and in the corners of the projected area.
  4. Lay the fabric flat, grain aligned, then trace the pieces with chalk or a washable marker, or cut directly along the lines.

Tips

  • Darken the room: the lines show up better.
  • Do not move the projector or the table once calibrated. If in doubt, measure the square again.
  • Press the fabric before laying it out: a crease distorts the lines.
