Béa dress

From ready-to-wear to made to measure: start from your size, then refine as much as you like.

Difficulty●●○○

A loose summer dress, easy to sew, in a light linen or cotton. The necklines are trimmed with a piece of contrasting fabric, such as a wax print, which is echoed on the welt pockets.

If your printer shrinks the print despite “100 %” (e.g. a 48 mm square), enter the measured value: the PDF will be enlarged to compensate.

Tip: do a first test in cheap fabric (an old sheet, for example).


The one-page PDF contains all the pieces at 1:1 scale, without being split into A4 sheets: it is meant to be projected onto the fabric (for example with Pattern Projector).

For a successful print or projection, see Printing at the right size.
