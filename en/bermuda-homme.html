Safari Bermuda shorts, men / women

Utility shorts, calculated from your measurements.

A short version of the safari trousers: cargo pockets, back pockets, a button fly and turned-up cuffs at the hem. The pattern is built on FreeSewing’s Titan trouser block (MIT licence).

Difficulty●●●●

Enter your measurements, choose the options and download the pattern as an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for projector sewing. Everything is calculated in your browser; none of your data is sent to me.

To take your measurements correctly, see the Measurement guide tab. All vertical measurements start from the same waist line.

The one-page PDF contains all the pieces at 1:1 scale, not split into A4 sheets: it is meant to be projected onto the fabric (for example with Pattern Projector).
