About

This site offers free parametric patterns for patch pockets and other garment details: you enter your dimensions, the site calculates the pattern in your browser and generates an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale. No data is sent to this site, nor stored.

Credit

Patterns originally designed with Seamly2D, free parametric pattern-making software.

Your feedback

The “Give feedback” link at the bottom of each page opens a bilingual (French/English) form hosted by a third-party service. If you use it, the information you enter voluntarily (rating, comment) is sent to that service and read by the author of the site. The patterns you create are never sent.
