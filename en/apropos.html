About

This site offers free parametric patterns for a safari jacket, hats (bucket hat and beret) and garment details (patch pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs, cartridge holders): you enter your measurements, the site calculates the pattern in your browser and generates an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale. No data is sent to this site, nor stored.

A word from the author

I am retired and took up sewing late in life, all on my own, following tutorials on the web.

It was by using FreeSewing’s free parametric patterns (freesewing.org), then drafting with Seamly2D (free parametric pattern-making software), and making safari jackets from my grandmother’s old linen-cotton (métis) sheets, that I got interested in studying the pocket patterns and other details presented here.

This site was born from an observation: all I found on the web were isolated pocket patterns, no systematic set, and few options. So I decided to organise and format the results of my pattern work to share them free of charge through this site. It does not, of course, claim to be exhaustive on the pattern-making of these details.

The safari jacket (saharienne) is the common thread of the garment-detail patterns published on this site: the pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs and cartridge holders all come from my safari jackets. My safari jacket pattern, with or without lining, is included here, along with my bucket hat and beret patterns. Almost all of these patterns are parametric, so you can make them to your own measurements. I also intend to add other garment details useful for jackets and coats.

If you have particular requests within the scope of this site, please let me know through the “Your feedback” form (anonymous) or through the “Contact” section below.
