About

This site offers free parametric patterns for a safari jacket, hats (bucket hat and beret) and garment details (patch pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs, cartridge holders): you enter your measurements, the site calculates the pattern in your browser and generates an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale. No data is sent to this site, nor stored. The site is free, with no ads and no sign-up. I don’t sell anything.

Who these patterns are for

They are meant for hobbyists who sew for themselves, with a little experience or a wish to learn. Because the patterns are calculated from your own measurements, they suit women and men alike; the pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs and hats can be sewn onto any garment.

The site starts from what I have sewn myself: there are no trousers, skirts or dresses yet. If you miss another kind of pattern, let me know through the “Your feedback” form.

A word from the author

I am retired and took up sewing late in life, all on my own, following tutorials on the web.

It was by using FreeSewing’s free parametric patterns (freesewing.org), then drafting with Seamly2D (free parametric pattern-making software), and making safari jackets from my grandmother’s old linen-cotton (métis) sheets, that I got interested in studying the pocket patterns and other details presented here.

This site was born from an observation: all I found on the web were isolated pocket patterns, no systematic set, and few options. So I decided to organise and format the results of my pattern work to share them free of charge through this site. It does not, of course, claim to be exhaustive on the pattern-making of these details.

The safari jacket (saharienne) is the common thread of the garment-detail patterns published on this site: the pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs and cartridge holders all come from my safari jackets. My safari jacket pattern, with or without lining, is included here, along with my bucket hat and beret patterns. Almost all of these patterns are parametric, so you can make them to your own measurements. I also intend to add other garment details useful for jackets and coats.

If you have particular requests within the scope of this site, please let me know through the “Your feedback” form (anonymous) or through the “Contact” section below.

Your projects on the site

Have you sewn one of my patterns? Send a photo to the contact address, with:

  • the pattern and the measurements you chose;
  • the fabric, if you like;
  • the caption you would like, with your first name, a nickname or no name at all.

I reply to every message, usually within a week. If I choose your photo, I will suggest a caption, and nothing is published without your written consent.

You decide what the photo shows, including your face. If other people appear in it, make sure they agree (a parent's consent for a child).

Before publishing, I crop and resize the image and remove its hidden data (location, camera). Published photos follow the site's licence (CC BY-NC 4.0). You can ask me to remove them at any time, by email.

Suggest a pattern

Do you design your own patterns? I am happy to look at them. All my patterns are made to measure: each one is turned into calculations that adapt to every visitor's measurements. So I publish, at my discretion, the patterns I can adapt this way with reasonable effort.

What you can send:

  • a Seamly2D (.sm2d) or Valentina (.val) file, with its measurements and formulas: the easiest to adapt;
  • a pattern as a PDF, scanned or hand-drawn, with its measurements;
  • a simple idea for a design: a sketch, a photo, a description. It may inspire a future pattern.

What I ask:

  • the pattern must be your own design, not a copy or adaptation of a commercial pattern;
  • your agreement to publish it for free, under the site's licence (CC BY-NC 4.0);
  • how you would like to be credited: your name, a nickname, or not at all.

I reply to every suggestion. I cannot publish them all, and adapting an accepted pattern may take several weeks.
