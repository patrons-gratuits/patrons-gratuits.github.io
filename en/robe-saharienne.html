Safari dress

From ready-to-wear to made-to-measure: start from your size and refine as much as you like.

The safari jacket turned into a dress: a military-inspired utility shirt dress, in the line of the safari jacket that couture borrowed from the uniform.

Difficulty●●●○

Choose the style, enter your measurements, choose the pieces and download the pattern as A4 sheets at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for projection. Everything is calculated in your browser; none of your data is sent to me.

To take your measurements correctly, see the Measurement guide tab.
