Safari jacket

From ready-to-wear to made to measure: start from your size, then refine as much as you like.

A military-inspired utility safari jacket, to sew in a lightweight cloth for summer or a sturdier one for mid-season.

Difficulty●●●○

Choose your style, enter your measurements, choose the pieces and download the pattern as A4 pages at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for video projection. Everything is calculated in your browser; none of your data is sent to me.

To take your measurements correctly, see the Measurement guide tab.
