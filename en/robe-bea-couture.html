Béa dress: sewing guide

Guide in progress: it is still being written and may change in the coming days. The pattern itself is final.

This guide gives the assembly order for the Béa dress. The pocket steps are shown according to your choice.

Your pockets:

Supplies

  • Main fabric: light linen, cotton or linen blend, plain. In 140 cm (55 in) width, about:
    • mini: 1.80 m;
    • midi: 2.20 m;
    • maxi: 2.80 m.
    The front and back are cut on the fold: lay one above the other.
  • Trim fabric: about 30 cm of a wax print or patterned fabric, for the neckline trims, and for the welt and the facing of the welt pockets.
  • Secondary fabric, for the facings: about 50 cm, your choice of the dress fabric, a thin cotton or the trim fabric. It is there to lift the style of the dress: a colour contrast and a touch of fun, glimpsed at the edge of the necklines and armholes when you move.
  • Light fusible interfacing: a small piece, for the welt pockets only.

Transfer the marks: darts, notches, pocket openings, hem fold lines.

With welt pockets, the notches on the sides of the front and back are not needed: ignore them.

The front is a little longer than the back: this is normal. The bust dart takes up this length at the side.

Assembly

Prepare

Stay-stitch 0.8 cm from the edge along the necklines and armholes of the front and back, so that they do not stretch out of shape.

The bust darts

Fold each dart right sides together, matching the notches. Stitch from the edge towards the point, finishing right at the fold. Tie the threads at the point without backstitching. Press the dart downwards.

The welt pockets

  1. Fuse the interfacing to the wrong side of the front, under the opening outline.
  2. Stitch the facing (trim fabric) to the top of the “Pocket bag” piece, right sides together, the bottom turned in 1 cm, following the “Garniture” mark. This way, when the pocket opens, you see the trim fabric rather than the bag.
  3. Attach the welt and the bags by following this video, which shows a single welt pocket set at an angle: How to Sew an Angled Single Welt Pocket – Cashmerette.

Practise on a scrap of fabric before cutting into the dress: this is the only tricky step of the pattern.

The neckline trims

  1. Place the front trim on the bottom of the front neckline, right sides together, and stitch at 1 cm.
  2. Press the seam towards the trim.
  3. Do the same for the back trim, at the bottom of the back neckline.

The facings

  1. Overlock the free (bottom) edge of both facings, or turn it in 0.5 cm and stitch.
  2. Place the front facing on the front, right sides together, leaving the shoulders and sides open. Stitch at 1 cm along the neckline (trim included) and both armholes.
  3. Trim the seam allowances to 0.5 cm, clip the curves and the corners of the neckline without cutting the stitching.
  4. Turn the facing to the wrong side, push out the corners and press, rolling the seam slightly towards the inside.
  5. If possible, understitch: stitch the facing right next to the seam, through the seam allowances, so that it does not roll out.
  6. Do the same at the back.

The shoulders

  1. Unfold the facings. Join the front and back shoulders, right sides together, without catching the facings. Press the seams open.
  2. Turn the facing shoulder allowances in towards each other and close them by hand with a slip stitch.

The side pockets

  1. Stitch a pocket bag to each side of the front and back, right sides together, between the notches, at 0.5 cm from the edge.
  2. Press the bags outwards.

The side seams

  1. Unfold the facings. Place the front on the back, right sides together, notches and armhole seams facing each other.
  2. Stitch each side in one go, from the bottom of the facing to the hem of the dress.
    • With side pockets: stitch up to the first notch, go around the bag, then pick up the side seam again at the second notch.
  3. Overlock the seam allowances together, or separately if you press the seam open.
  4. Fold the facings to the wrong side and attach them to the side seams with a few hand stitches.

The hem

  1. Fold 1.5 cm to the wrong side, then 4 cm following the fold line. Press.
  2. Stitch by machine close to the edge of the fold, or blind-stitch for a discreet hem.

Finishing

Give a last press, especially the necklines, armholes and darts.

Tips

  • Make a test version first in cheap fabric (an old sheet, for example), above all to check the length and the depth of the necklines.
  • For a wax print: centre a motif on the front trim, as this is what shows most.
  • Back neckline: if you wear a bra, do not lower it by more than about 6 cm.
