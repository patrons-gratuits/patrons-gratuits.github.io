Lined safari jacket

This pattern comes with or without a shoulder piece: choose first with the box below. Then enter your measurements, choose the pieces and download the pattern as A4 pages at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for video projection. Everything is calculated in your browser; none of your data is sent to us.

Not sure how to take a measurement? See the Measurement guide tab.

If your printer shrinks the page despite “100 %” (e.g. a 48 mm square), enter the measured value: the PDF will be enlarged to compensate.


The one-page PDF has all the pieces at 1:1 scale, without being split into A4 sheets: it is meant to be projected onto the fabric (for example with Pattern Projector). The 5 × 5 cm square is used to calibrate the projection.

To print at the right scale and check the 5 × 5 cm test square, see Printing correctly.
