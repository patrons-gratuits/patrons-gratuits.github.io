How it works

  1. Choose a pattern family
  2. Patch pockets, epaulettes and sleeve tabs: choose the shape, then enter your measurements and the seam allowance. Cartridge holder: the dimensions are fixed.
  3. Download the PDF and print it at 100 %, checking the 5 cm test square (guide)
