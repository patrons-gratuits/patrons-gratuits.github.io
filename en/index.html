How it works

  1. 1Choose your style.The pattern, then its options: sleeves, pockets, flaps… A drawing shows you the result.
  2. 2Enter your measurements.One at a time, with an explanatory illustration. They are saved on your device, and nowhere else.
  3. 3Download and sew.An A4 PDF to print at 100%, or a one-page PDF for projection, with the sewing guide.
  4. 4Share.Sewing for someone far away? They enter their measurements on the pattern page and send them to you in one click with “Share”: you open the pattern already set up. Learn more

The site in brief. Free parametric sewing patterns: safari jackets to design yourself, unlined or lined, for men and women; two dresses, the Béa dress and the safari dress; safari Bermuda shorts; hats (bucket hat, panel beret, Basque beret); and garment details (patch pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs, pen and glasses holders), with over 100 pocket combinations. You choose your style and enter your measurements: the pattern is calculated in your browser and downloaded as an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for projection. A test square lets you check the print.
