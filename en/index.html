The site at a glance

Free parametric patterns: a made-to-measure safari jacket, hats (bucket hat and berets) and garment details (26 basic shapes, all adjustable: patch pockets (over 100 combinations with gussets, openings and flaps), epaulettes, sleeve tabs, cartridge holders). Enter your measurements and choose your options: the pattern is calculated in your browser and downloaded as an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for video projection. A test square lets you correct the print if your printer reduces slightly.
