The site in brief. Free parametric sewing patterns: a safari jacket to design yourself, unlined or lined, a summer dress, hats (bucket hat and berets) and garment details (patch pockets, epaulettes, sleeve tabs, pen and glasses holders), with 26 basic shapes and over 100 pocket combinations. You choose your style and enter your measurements: the pattern is calculated in your browser and downloaded as an A4 PDF at 1:1 scale, or as a single page for projection. A test square lets you check the print.
