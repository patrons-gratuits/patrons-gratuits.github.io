Cartridge holder: pen and glasses holder

An original design made for my safari jackets (sahariennes): a “conversion” of the cartridge pocket found on military and hunting safari jackets. Practical, this pocket is a holder for pens and a pair of glasses. It is applied to the left side of the jacket's chest. It can be classic, made in the main fabric, or fancy, using eye-catching fabrics for this original detail.

Two versions with fixed dimensions, to download as A4 PDFs at 1:1 scale: the cartridge holder with a pocket (12 × 12 cm / 4.72 × 4.72 in) and the simplified version, without a pocket (12 × 5 cm / 4.72 × 1.97 in). Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Cartridge holder with pocket

It includes the bottom pocket, which holds the pens and stops them from slipping. Finished size: 12 × 12 cm (4.72 × 4.72 in).

Simplified cartridge holder

The same holder without the pocket. Finished size: 12 × 5 cm (4.72 × 1.97 in). The sewing steps are the same as the version with a pocket, minus the pocket (see the instructions below).

The 8 red lines on the Main piece are buttonholes: the ribbon passes through them to hold the pens and the glasses. The blue dashes are fold lines. There is no seam allowance: the 1.5 cm (0.6 in) fold-under allowance takes its place.
